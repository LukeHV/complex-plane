How to use the grapher

Type one thing per line on the left. Click any grey example below to add it to your graph.

Numbers & points

Any complex number is plotted as a point.

3 + 4i 2e^(iπ/3) cis(π/4)

Give it a name to reuse it. Named numbers can be dragged on the graph.

a = 1 + 2i b = -2 + i a + b a b conj(a)

Loci (equations in z)

Use z for the moving point.

Circle: |z - 1 - i| = 2

Perpendicular bisector: |z - 2| = |z + 2i|

Half-line: arg(z - 1) = π/4

Ellipse: |z - 2| + |z + 2| = 6

Arc: arg(z - 2) - arg(z + 2) = π/3

Lines: Re(z) = 3 y = 2x + 1

Regions (inequalities)

Use < > ≤ ≥, chain them, and join with and / or.

|z| ≤ 2 1 < |z - i| < 2

|z| < 3 and 0 ≤ arg(z) ≤ π/3

|z - 1| < |z + 1|

Strict inequalities get a dashed edge, ≤ and ≥ a solid one.

Solving equations

A complex equation shows its solutions as points.

z^5 = 1 z^2 = -3 + 4i z^3 + z + 1 = 0

Where two loci meet – join two equations with and:

|z - 1| = 2 and arg(z) = π/4

Curves & functions

Parametric curve in t (or θ): z = 2e^(it) t e^(it)

Functions: f(z) = z^2 + 1 then |f(z)| = 2

A bare expression in z draws its domain colouring: (z^2 - 1)/(z^2 + 1)

Sliders

A name set to a plain number becomes a slider. Press ▶ to animate it, and + i to give it an imaginary part.

r = 1.5 then |z - i| = r

A named complex number gets two sliders, either Re / Im or modulus / argument; switch between them under the line.

c = 1 + 2i w = 2e^(πi/3)

If you use a letter that isn't defined yet, click add slider under the line.

Handy to know

  • Functions: Re Im arg |z| conj sqrt exp ln sin cos tan cis
  • pi → π, <= → ≤, >= → ≥ as you type. 45° works for angles.
  • x and y mean Re(z) and Im(z).
  • arg gives the principal value in (−π, π].
  • Enter adds a new line, ↑ ↓ move between lines.
  • Click a coloured dot to hide a line or change its colour. Hover a line to pick out its graph.
  • On the graph: arrow keys pan, + / − zoom, 0 resets the view.
  • Your graph is saved in this browser automatically. Use Share to pass it on.
